# 💬 Advanced Buyer-Seller Chat System (UI/UX Upgrades)

Comprehensive, production-ready **Buyer-Seller Chat Experience** for the Campus Marketplace.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & UX Capabilities

### 1. `ChatPage.tsx` (1-on-1 Real-Time Chat & Negotiation)
- **📍 Interactive Campus Meetup Coordinator**:
  - Modal sheet with **5 pre-screened safe campus meetup zones** (Campus Library Main Lobby, Student Union Cafe, Science Quad Plaza, Dining Commons West Entrance, Campus Main Gate Security Booth).
  - Quick meeting time presets (e.g. `Today at 12:30 PM (Lunch)`, `Today at 3:30 PM (After class)`).
  - Renders an interactive **Campus Meetup Card** in the chat thread.
  - Recipient can tap **"Accept Meetup Spot"** with 1 click to confirm the meetup.
- **🏷️ Interactive Price Offer Proposal**:
  - **"Make Offer"** modal with quick student discount chips (`-5%`, `-10%`, `-15%`, `-20%`) or custom dollar input.
  - Live calculated savings (`"You save $5.00 (11% off)"`).
  - Renders a styled **Price Offer Card** in the conversation stream with accept action for sellers.
- **🛡️ Campus Safety Modal & Tips**:
  - Safety notice advising students on safe daytime public exchanges.
  - Dedicated Campus Safety Protocol guide popup.
- **✨ Message Reactions & Delivery Status**:
  - Quick reaction picker (`👍`, `❤️`, `🤝`, `📍`, `💰`, `🎉`) attached to messages.
  - Double checkmarks (`✓✓`) for delivery and read status.
- **📌 Pinned Product Context Bar**:
  - Item preview with title, listing price pill, and campus location tag.
  - Active Deal status banner (displays confirmed meetup or accepted offer right under header).
- **⚡ Suggestions Carousel**:
  - Quick question prompt chips tailored for buyers & sellers.

---

### 2. `MessagesPage.tsx` (Inbox & Inquiries Center)
- **🔍 Instant Smart Search**:
  - Search across buyer/seller names, item titles, message snippets, and meetup locations with instant clear button.
- **🗂️ Multi-Perspective Filter Tabs**:
  - `All chats`: Complete conversation history.
  - `Buying`: Items you are inquiring to buy from other students.
  - `Selling`: Customer inquiries on your listings.
  - `Meetups`: Chats with planned or confirmed campus meetups.
  - `Unread`: Conversations with unread incoming messages.
- **📌 Conversation Card Actions**:
  - Pin conversations to the top (`📌`).
  - Mark as read / unread.
  - Archive / Delete conversations with confirmation prompt.
- **📊 Activity Summary Header**:
  - Live counts of total conversations, active meetups, and pending offers.
- **🌱 Micro-interactions & Visual Polish**:
  - Dynamic avatar background generated by name hash.
  - Green active online indicator.
  - Verified student ID badge.
  - Relative timestamp formatting (`"Just now"`, `"5m ago"`, `"Yesterday"`).
